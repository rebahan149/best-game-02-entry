Gleamward

The dark sends its shadows for your hearth, seven nights running. Your only weapon is light itself: drag the mirror shards, twist them, and bend the lantern's beam into a burning web the shadows must cross.

  • Drag a mirror to move it
  • Mouse wheel, Q / E, or the rotate buttons to turn the selected mirror
  • Drag the lantern (or A / D) to aim the beam
  • F flares the beam (5 embers, double damage, brief surge)
  • P pauses · Shadows that burn drop embers — spend them at dawn
